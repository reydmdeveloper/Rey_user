How it works:

  • Step 1: The engine opens Adobe FrameMaker in the background and exports the source .fm binary document to Interchange .mif format.
  • Step 2: The Python translation engine parses your Word document .docx, reads the translation table, strips clean text keys, resolves formatting tags (like fonts, styles, variables), and translates the MIF content.
  • Step 3: FrameMaker imports the translated MIF and compiles it back into a native binary .fm file ready for download.
Note: Adobe FrameMaker 2022 must be installed and active on the host machine to execute compilation steps.
